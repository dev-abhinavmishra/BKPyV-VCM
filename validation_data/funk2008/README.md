# Digitized patient plasma BKV series — Funk et al. 2008

Source: Figure 3 of Funk GA, Gosert R, Comoli P, Ginevri F, Hirsch HH.
"Polyomavirus BK Replication Dynamics In Vivo and In Silico to Predict
Cytopathology and Viral Clearance in Kidney Transplants."
Am J Transplant. 2008;8(11):2368-2377. doi:10.1111/j.1600-6143.2008.02402.x
License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (open archive). Digitized programmatically from the
published figure (pixel-calibrated to the figure's dashed reference lines at
log10 4 and 7); precision ~+/-0.15 log10 and ~+/-0.5 week. Filled diamonds =
plasma BKV load; open squares (not extracted here) = urine BKV load.

Detection limit in the source figure: 500 copies/mL (log10 ~2.7); values at
or below it were recorded by the authors as 100 copies/mL (log10 2.0).
Patients shown are 6 representative cases of 31 longitudinally monitored
kidney-transplant recipients (2002-2008, Basel + Genova).

Columns: week post-transplant, log10 plasma copies/mL.
