FIG. 1  ·  the problem
Clinical reality

Every kidney transplant carries a sleeping virus.
Today, we wait for it to wake up.

BK polyomavirus reactivates in 30–60% of recipients. In 1–10% it destroys the graft. There is no antiviral — the only lever is reducing immunosuppression, which trades viral control for rejection risk.
Q1 — whenwhen will viremia start in this patient?
Q2 — outcomewill this viral load clear — or plateau into nephropathy?
Q3 — howtaper drugs, or convert to mTOR — and when?
Consensus management (Hirsch 2016, AST-IDCOP): screen monthly, react at 10⁴ copies/mL. The whole protocol is reactive.

FIG. 3  ·  the model
Architecture — 23 state variables, five layers
The graft is not one well-mixed pot. Virus lives in tissue, drains to urine, hides in a latent reservoir, spreads cell-to-cell — and each copy carries a genotype that can spontaneously rearrange.

FIG. 5  ·  emergent result — 1 of 8
Optimal-control analysis

Convert, don't taper.
The optimizer found it — nobody told it to.

Tapering keeps the virus in a high-replication window for weeks longer. Converting to sirolimus removes S-phase permissiveness and restores immune control — the mechanistic why, in one picture.
emergent · not fitted taper keeps ~10⁴·⁴ plateau
FIG. 6  ·  emergent result — 2 of 8
NCCR quasi-species — the part Dr. Thompson suggested

The rearranged clone concentrates in plasma
before it dominates urine — matching Gosert 2008.

Rearranged NCCR overexpresses T-antigen but pays a capsid cost — so it wins where production matters and loses where virion output does. Two pools make the direction emerge: the plasma pool’s rearranged share runs ~2.7× the urine pool’s.
plasma : urine ≈ 2.7× rr share testable on paired urine+plasma data
FIG. 7  ·  emergent result — 3 of 8
Stochastic reactivation — 150 virtual patients per trough

Not “onset at week 6.”
A distribution — and it moves with the trough.

Higher troughs give earlier, tighter onset — the clinical 4–16-week cluster lands inside the simulated distribution. This is the “which kidney reactivates?” question, answered as a probability density.
median ≈6.3 wk @ tac 8 ng/mL
